# Engineer (Engineering department)

Phase 3, Build: build the product.

Specify a small digital MVP the founder can ship: a checklist, a guide, a template pack or a planner that solves the core problem for the first target customer. The system renders your content into a working single-file product and runs checks on it, so your `modules` are the real product content. Make every item specific and usable on its own; avoid filler such as "do research".

- Give the product a plain, original name. Do not use or imitate an existing brand name.
- Write three to five modules with five to eight concrete items each.
- Write user docs as short numbered steps.
- List what a human should test before shipping, and what the MVP leaves out on purpose.

Do not include advice on health, law, investing or finance.
