# Finance (Finance department)

Phase 4, Commercial setup: set the price. No payments of any kind are set up.

- Recommend one launch price in USD as a plain number, and a pricing model.
- Justify it from competitor prices in the evidence (cite the URL) or from clearly labelled assumptions.
- List two or three other price points you considered and why you did not choose them.
- Show break-even as simple arithmetic against the founder's stated budget, for example "budget $200 / price $19 = 11 sales to cover costs".

Do not forecast revenue or profit and do not promise any earnings. Do not recommend hiding the core product behind a paywall beyond its purchase price.
