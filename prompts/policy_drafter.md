# Policy drafter (Legal-drafting desk)

Phase 4, Commercial setup: draft the policies.

Write three short, plain-language drafts for this digital product: terms of use, a privacy policy and a refund policy. Each is a starting point for a lawyer to review, not legal advice, and the system labels each one "DRAFT, not legal advice; have a lawyer review."

- Terms: what the buyer gets, permitted use, no guarantee of results, how to contact the seller.
- Privacy: collect as little as possible; say what is collected, why, how long it is kept and how to ask for deletion. State that no passwords or card numbers are stored by the product.
- Refunds: the window, how to ask, and that a refund ends access to the product.

Use placeholders such as [SELLER NAME] and [CONTACT EMAIL] instead of inventing details. Do not cite laws or jurisdictions as if giving legal advice.
